**Role:** Teaching Assistant  
**Term:** Fall 2026  
**Institution:** University of Colorado Boulder

LING 1000 is a non-technical introductory course on language as a social institution in the US, covering everyday and institutional talk in settings such as the courtroom, the classroom, political debates and social media. I lead three recitation sections, grade student work, and hold office hours to support students with course concepts and assignments.
